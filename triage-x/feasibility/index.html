Feasibility Evidence

Supporting materials for autonomous casualty triage capability demonstrated by CMU Team Chiron in the DARPA Triage Challenge.

About this page

This page collects publicly releasable summaries, illustrations and video of capability demonstrated by CMU Team Chiron. Each entry links to a short document, an illustration, or a video.

Detailed technical reports and program data are not hosted here. Distribution-limited material, raw datasets and scored-run records are available to authorised reviewers on request — please contact darpa-triage@cmu.edu.
